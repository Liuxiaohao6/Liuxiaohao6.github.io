👋🏻 Welcome!

Hello! I am Zhihao Liu (刘至昊), a at . I work on trustworthy and privacy-preserving artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on the security, privacy, and reliability of large language models and AI agents.

My research spans LLM agent security, prompt injection defenses, retrieval-augmented generation security, machine unlearning, differential privacy, and privacy risks in modern language and vision-language models.

📬 I am open to research discussions and collaborations. Please feel free to contact me if our interests overlap.

🔎 Research Interests

  • LLM and Agent Security: Understanding and mitigating prompt injection, adversarial manipulation, and other security risks in tool-using language-model agents.
  • Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning: Developing practical differential privacy methods and studying privacy leakage in large language and vision-language models.
  • Machine Unlearning: Designing and evaluating methods that remove the influence of selected training data while preserving model utility.
  • Trustworthy Retrieval and Inference: Improving the security and reliability of retrieval-augmented generation, long-context inference, and model-serving systems.
